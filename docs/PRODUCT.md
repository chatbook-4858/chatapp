# Product Scope

## ChatApp by GMC Pvt LTD

- Direct and group conversations
- Text, image, video, document, voice, and location sharing
- Voice and video calls
- Status updates and disappearing content
- Search, notifications, and multi-device sessions
- Privacy controls, account security, blocking, and reporting

## Potential differentiators

- Urdu-first multilingual interface
- Scheduled messages
- Collaborative channels and business profiles
- Role-based team management and broadcast channels
- Optional message translation with consent
- Regional hosting choices and enterprise retention/export controls

## Demo limitations

The current static preview demonstrates interface behavior only. It does not provide real user accounts, cross-device chat delivery, actual audio/video calls, secure transport, or a production service. Those features need engineering, infrastructure, testing, and deployment.
